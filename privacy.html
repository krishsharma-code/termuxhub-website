How Termux Hub handles your data

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Who we are

Termux Hub is a free educational website that publishes Termux tutorials and cybersecurity tool guides. It is a personal project run by Krish Sharma, a Computer Engineering student in Delhi, India. You can reach the site owner at krish.krishsharma9376@gmail.com.

This page explains what information is collected when you visit the site, why it is collected, and how you can control it.

What we collect

Termux Hub has no accounts, no sign-up form and no comments section, so we never ask you for your name, address or payment details. The information that reaches us is limited to what browsers and advertising systems send automatically:

  • Usage data: pages you open, time spent, referring website or search engine, screen size, browser and operating system.
  • Approximate location: the city or region level only, derived from your IP address. We do not store your exact IP address.
  • Device storage: your light/dark theme choice and offline caching for the installable app version of the site.

Cookies and local storage

Cookies are small text files a website stores in your browser. Termux Hub itself sets only functional storage, used to remember your theme preference and to let the site work offline. Everything else comes from Google services described below:

  • Google Analytics 4 — sets cookies such as _ga and _ga_* so we can count visits and see which guides are useful.
  • Google AdSense — sets advertising cookies, including the DoubleClick cookie, so ads can be shown and measured.

You can delete or block cookies at any time in your browser settings. Blocking them does not stop you from reading any page on this site.

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand traffic in aggregate — for example, how many people read the Nmap guide, or which countries visitors come from. Reports we see are statistical and are not used to identify individual visitors. IP anonymisation is switched on. Google's own handling of this data is described in the Google Privacy Policy and on the "How Google uses information from sites that use our services" page.

To opt out of analytics tracking across all websites, you can install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Google AdSense and advertising

This site is supported by advertising served through Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and other sites on the internet.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can also opt out of third-party vendor cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info or youronlinechoices.com.

Advertisements are chosen by Google. We do not control which ads appear and we do not receive your personal data from advertisers.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what personal data is held about you, to have it corrected or deleted, or to object to processing. Since we hold no account data, most requests are handled by the browser-level cookie controls above. For anything else, write to krish.krishsharma9376@gmail.com and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Third-party links

Guides link to external sites such as GitHub, F-Droid and tool documentation. Once you follow such a link, that site's own privacy policy applies. We are not responsible for the content or data practices of external sites.

Children

This site is intended for students and professionals aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will remove it.

Security and data retention

The site is served over HTTPS. Analytics data is retained for the period set in the Google Analytics property settings (by default 14 months) and is then deleted automatically. We keep no other visitor records.

Educational disclaimer

All tutorials on Termux Hub are for education and authorised testing only. Always have permission before testing any system or network.

Changes to this policy

If the way the site handles data changes — for example if a new analytics or advertising tool is added — this page will be updated and the date at the top will change. Continued use of the site after an update means you accept the revised policy.
